Saddle Scribe Support

Saddle Scribe helps you keep the saddle notch for one pair of logs. Measure the widest gap, lock the opening as that gap plus a quarter inch, center both bubbles, and drag along the lower log. A bubble that leaves the center only scratches. Turning the opening clears the line. Roll the log only after a full line exists. Cut the edge to the line to seat the logs. Hollow past the line without passing half the diameter, or the notch opens again.

The notes stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
