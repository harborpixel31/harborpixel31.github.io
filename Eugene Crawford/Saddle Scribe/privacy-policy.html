Saddle Scribe Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Saddle Scribe is a free iOS app. It keeps one pair of logs on this iPhone: the widest gap, the opening, whether both bubbles stayed centered, and whether the logs are seated, hollowed, or the notch is too deep. It does not sell anything inside the app.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect a name, email, or location for the log notes themselves.

A pair you name, and the readings for that pair, stay in a file on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that file.
