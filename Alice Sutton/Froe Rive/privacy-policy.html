Froe Rive Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Froe Rive is a free iOS app. It keeps notes about splitting one oak bolt on this iPhone. It does not sell anything inside the app.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect a name, email, or location for the bolt notes themselves.

A bolt you name, and whether the froe is in the end, the bolt has opened, or the bolt has run out, stays in a file on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that file.
