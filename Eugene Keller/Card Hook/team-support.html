Card Hook Support

Card Hook keeps the hook on one card scraper. File the whole edge until it is square. Hone only after it is square. Draw the burr flat only after honing. A light lift turns the hook. One hard lift folds it. Scrape only while the hook is turned: a shaving comes off. A ragged burr or a folded hook only makes dust. File again to clear that and start from a square edge.

The cards stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
