Card Hook Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Card Hook is a free iOS app. It keeps each card scraper on this iPhone: the name, and whether the edge is square, honed, the burr is drawn, the hook is turned, the burr is ragged, the hook is folded, or a shaving came off. It does not sell anything inside the app.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect a name, email, or location for the card notes themselves.

A card you name, and the edge of that card, stay in a file on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that file.
