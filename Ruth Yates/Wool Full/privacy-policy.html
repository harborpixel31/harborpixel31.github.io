Wool Full Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026.

Wool Full keeps one wool batt on this iPhone. Soak it with warm soapy water, rub gently, and pinch until the whole sheet lifts. A throw before that leaves a thin spot. After the sheet holds, roll each new quarter turn until the batt is fulled. Added wool does not stay, and a rinse does not open it. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.

Contact address: United States.
