Wool Full Support

Effective date: November 27, 2026.

Wool Full is a free iPhone app for one wool batt. Soak, rub, pinch, throw, roll, and rinse stay on this phone. There is no account and nothing to buy. A dry batt can be deleted. A fulled batt can be renamed. Share writes whether the batt is fulled, still has a thin spot, or is still open.

Contact address: United States.
