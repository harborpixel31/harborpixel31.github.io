Oakum Drive Support

Oakum Drive keeps one wooden seam. Reef a foul seam before a strand goes in. Tuck one strand, then drive it. Four or five strands mean the seam is home. Finish only a seam that is already home. A drive after the finish clears the seam, and it has to be reefed again.

The seams stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
