Oakum Drive Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Oakum Drive is a free iOS app. It keeps each wooden seam on this iPhone: the name, how many strands are driven, whether a strand is tucked, whether the seam is finished, and whether it must be reefed. It does not sell anything inside the app.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect a name, email, or location for the seam notes themselves.

A seam you name, and the strands driven into it, stay in a file on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that file.
