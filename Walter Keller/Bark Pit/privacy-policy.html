Bark Pit Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026.

Bark Pit keeps one hide on this iPhone. A raw hide goes into the weak pit first until the grain colors. The same hide stays pale in the weak pit. A raw hide in the strong pit draws the grain. A colored hide in the strong pit turns brown through. Dragging on the cut does not change the hide. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.

Contact address: United States.
