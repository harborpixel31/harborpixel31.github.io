Spine Back Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026.

Spine Back keeps one book block on this iPhone. Drag until the sections are sewn, round the spine about a third, then hammer the shoulders until they are backed. Hammering a flat spine bruises it. Hammering a backed shoulder square splits the joint. A thin block will not take a round or a hammer. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.
