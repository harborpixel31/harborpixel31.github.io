Hawser Lay Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Hawser Lay is a free iOS app. It keeps each hawser on this iPhone: the name, the circumference, the lay, whether the hands are opposite, and whether the rope is coiled. It does not sell anything inside the app.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect a name, email, or location for the rope notes themselves.

A rope you name, and the lay of that rope, stay in a file on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that file.
