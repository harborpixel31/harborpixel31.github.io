Hawser Lay Support

Hawser Lay keeps the lay of one hawser. Set the circumference first. Drag the lay until it meets the closed band. The same hand opens the lay. Only a closed lay can be coiled. A long lay, a short lay, or an open lay stays uncoiled.

The ropes stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
