Stake Turn Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026.

Stake Turn keeps one basket rim on this iPhone. Kink a wet stake in the band above the weave, then turn it down in front of the next two uprights and behind the one after. A dry stake snaps and stays snapped. Soaking a dry upright stake lets it bend. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.

Contact address: United States.
