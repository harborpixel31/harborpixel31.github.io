Toe Last Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Toe Last is a free iOS app. It keeps shoe-upper notes on this iPhone. It does not sell anything inside the app.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect a name, email, or location for the lasting notes themselves.

An upper you name, and whether it is damp, tacked, cracked, or crooked, stays in a file on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that file.
