Bowl Raise Support

Bowl Raise keeps one copper disc on this iPhone. Anneal it until it is soft, then raise only the next ring from the center. A hard disc does not move. A blow outside that ring does not count. A blow back onto a raised ring doubles the edge, and the edge stays. Planish only where the stake touches after the wall is formed. A fresh hard disc can be deleted. A planished disc can be renamed.
