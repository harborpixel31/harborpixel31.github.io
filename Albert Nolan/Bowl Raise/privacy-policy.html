Bowl Raise Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026.

Bowl Raise keeps one copper disc on this iPhone. Anneal it until the disc is soft, then raise only the next ring from the center. A hard disc does not move. A blow outside that ring does not count. A blow back onto a ring that has already risen doubles the edge, and that edge stays. Planishing before the wall is formed leaves a mark. When the wall is formed, anneal again and planish where the stake touches. A later raise does not move a planished wall. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.
